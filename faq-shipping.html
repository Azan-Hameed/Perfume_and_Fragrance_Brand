CLIENT SERVICE

Shipping & Returns

All Maison Zayn orders are carefully hand-packed in protective archival presentation boxes and ship within 2 business days. We offer complimentary express shipping on orders over $150.

Estimated transit is 3 to 7 business days depending on destination, with tracked courier updates provided from dispatch to arrival.

Unused, sealed items in their original cellophane wrapping and presentation box can be returned within 14 days of delivery.

Once your return is received and inspected by our atelier team, funds are refunded to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

We include one complimentary 2ml sample vial with every creation so you may test the fragrance on skin first before unsealing your full flacon.
